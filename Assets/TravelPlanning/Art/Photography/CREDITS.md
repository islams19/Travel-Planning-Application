# Bundled travel photography

These are illustrative travel photographs, not pictures of the fictional hotels or other seeded businesses. Photographs are bundled locally; no image service is called by the application. All three source files carry CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication on their Wikimedia Commons description pages. Unity imports them at a maximum texture size of 2048 pixels, with no mipmaps and high-quality compression.
